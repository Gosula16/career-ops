What it does

Everything needed to run a disciplined search from one repo.

Role evaluation

Score jobs with structured fit criteria instead of gut feel and scattered notes.

Tailored CV PDFs

Generate ATS-friendly resume variants tuned to each role and company context.

Portal scanning

Search configured companies and boards across Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, and more.

Batch processing

Review multiple listings in parallel and keep the queue moving without losing quality.

Tracker integrity

Normalize statuses, deduplicate entries, and keep one source of truth for the pipeline.

Interview prep

Accumulate reusable STAR stories and company-specific notes as you evaluate opportunities.

Workflow

Paste a job URL. Let the system route the rest.

  1. Detect the role archetype and evaluate fit against your CV.
  2. Produce a report, tailored PDF, and tracker update.
  3. Use scan, batch, apply, and interview-prep modes as the search deepens.

Quick start

Run it locally in minutes.

Install

git clone https://github.com/Gosula16/career-ops.git
cd career-ops
npm install
npx playwright install chromium

Configure

copy config\profile.example.yml config\profile.yml
copy templates\portals.example.yml portals.yml

Add your `cv.md`, then personalize the system through your preferred AI CLI.

Open source

Use the repo, adapt the modes, and make the workflow yours.

This site is only the front door. The real power lives in the local command center: your profile, your CV, your tracker, and your chosen AI assistant.
